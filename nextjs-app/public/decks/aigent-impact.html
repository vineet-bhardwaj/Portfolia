03 · The Implementation Service

The report tells the agent what to do. We help them do it.

A score and a report alone won't move the needle if the agent doesn't have time or expertise to execute. The AIgent Impact service includes hands-on implementation work delivered alongside the monthly report.

01

Listing Audit & NAP Cleanup

We audit the agent's presence across 20+ directories, identify every inconsistency in name, address, phone, hours, and product list, and submit corrections to each platform.

Deliverable · Updated listings across Google, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, Nextdoor, Yahoo Local, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and 12+ directories.
02

Bio & Profile Rewriting

We replace the generic template bio with a personalized, locally-rooted narrative that surfaces the agent's languages, story, specialties, and community ties — written to be both AI-readable and brand-compliant.

Deliverable · New bio for agents.allstate.com, Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Nextdoor.
03

Review Acquisition Campaigns

We design and execute monthly review acquisition campaigns — SMS templates, email sequences, in-office QR cards, and post-claim follow-ups — to systematically grow review volume and velocity.

Deliverable · Email/SMS templates, QR code materials, monthly tracking of new reviews acquired across platforms.
04

Google Business Profile Optimization

Monthly GBP optimization including photo uploads, weekly posts, Q&A engagement, attribute updates, and reputation responses. This is the single highest-leverage off-platform asset for AI visibility.

Deliverable · 4 GBP posts per month, 5 new photos, 100% Q&A response rate, all reviews responded to.
05

Local Press & Awards Outreach

We identify local "Best of" awards, business journal honors, and chamber recognition opportunities in each agent's market — and submit nomination packages, vote-drive campaigns, and PR pitches.

Deliverable · Quarterly award nominations, press release pitches, vote campaigns coordinated with the agent's customer base.
06

Referral Partnership Sourcing

We identify and outreach to local businesses that represent referral opportunities for each agent's product mix — car dealers, real estate agents, property managers, mortgage brokers — providing partnership templates and intro emails.

Deliverable · Curated list of 10+ local partnership prospects per quarter with intro email templates.
07

Community Visibility Building

We coordinate the agent's involvement with local chambers of commerce, charitable organizations, and community events — making sure each involvement is documented and indexed online for maximum AI visibility impact.

Deliverable · Quarterly community engagement calendar, sponsorship coordination, online documentation of all activities.
08

Monthly Score Refresh & Tracking

Each month, we re-run the full prompt corpus across all four AI engines, rescore the agent across all four pillars, and produce the updated report — so every month the agent sees measurable progress.

Deliverable · Updated monthly report, dashboard refresh, trajectory tracking, year-over-year comparison.
